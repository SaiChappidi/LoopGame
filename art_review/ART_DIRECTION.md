# Visual production direction

Preserve all seven runtime contracts, simulation state and collision coordinates.

- Stack Studio: warm limestone gallery, indigo distance, bronze inlay, glazed architectural slabs. Beveled edges, contact shadows and settling motion communicate mass.
- Skyline Sprint: blue-hour night market, terracotta and teal shopfronts, fabric awnings, lanterns, restrained signage. Courier with tailored backpack, scarf and articulated stride. Keep hazards brighter than scenery.
- Small World: miniature garden plaza with stone paths, flower beds, tiny shops and toy citizens. Mint scouts, coral rivals, neutral cream townsfolk; faces and flags improve recognition.
- Coastline: illustrated coastal rally. Ocean terraces, cliff silhouettes, palms and guardrails; projected road and detailed touring cars. Preserve screen-space steering accuracy at the player's collision line.
- Chromatic: a kinetic color instrument with precise rails, engraved calibration marks, segmented gates and clear redundant symbols.
- Soft Numbers: warm ceramic tiles in a walnut tray, inset cells, centered typography and sliding/merge motion.
- Cell Odyssey: microscopic tide pool, fine suspended organisms, membrane rims and organelles; preserve configurable creator colors.

All new vector art and procedural geometry are original project work. No external asset dependencies. Captures use deterministic seed 812 and isolated review saves. Before-source is ignored by Godot.
