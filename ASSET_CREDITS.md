# Asset source and license record

New gameplay visuals use original procedural artwork and geometry authored for this project, plus three original AI-generated background illustrations commissioned for Lantern Trail. No downloaded or paid assets were used.

- assets/citizens/neutral.svg, scout.svg, rival.svg: original illustrated toy citizens; vector source included. No third-party licensing requirements.
- games/stack.gd: original gallery architecture, stone terrace, beveled slab detailing, contact shadows and offcut animation.
- games/runner.gd: original market facade details, shop signage, striped awnings, lanterns, planters, benches, balconies, courier and tram additions. Existing project geometry retained and extended.
- games/crowd.gd: original garden plaza, planting, paths, shop roofs, flags and recruitment feedback.
- games/racer.gd: original projected coastal road, mountains, ocean marks, palms, guardrails and vehicle details.
- games/merge.gd: original ceramic tile and wooden tray rendering, slide and merge presentation.
- games/creator_arena.gd: original membrane, organelle and suspended micro-organism drawings. Creator-specified colors retained.
- assets/previews/*.png: captures of the actual updated Godot gameplay; generated by tests/visual_review.gd. Not external promotional artwork.
- assets/platformer_meadow.png, assets/platformer_orchard.png, assets/platformer_coast.png: original background illustrations generated with OpenAI ImageGen for Lantern Trail on 2026-10-07. Prompts requested three distinct wide storybook landscapes (golden meadow, lantern orchard at blue hour, and dawn coast), with no characters, logos, text, or third-party references. Integrated as level backdrops; no outside asset source.
- games/prism_stack.gd and games/lantern_trail.gd: original game UI, tetromino materials, characters, enemies, coins, platforms, props and effects drawn in Godot. No external gameplay assets.
- games/geometry_dash.gd: original neon precision-runner course, geometric hazards, cube runner, skyline, parallax grid and feedback, authored in Godot.
- games/rolling_sky.gd: original projected cloud course, ceramic-glass lanes, sky ridges, collectible light, hazards and rolling-ball artwork, authored in Godot.
- games/piano_tiles.gd: original four-lane piano cabinet, falling-key tiles, timing chart, key highlights and hit/miss feedback, authored in Godot.
- assets/music/neon_ascent.wav, glass_horizon.wav and amber_sonata.wav: original 30–34 second synthesized game scores generated from the included `tools/generate_original_soundtracks.py` using Python and NumPy. No samples, third-party compositions, or external music services are used. The scores are authored specifically for Pulse Run, Cloudroll and Keylight.
- Typography: existing Godot ThemeDB fallback font; no new font files added. Engine and bundled font licensing remains governed by the existing Godot distribution. Godot's license information is available through its About dialog and official distribution.

No relicensing of the pre-existing project or engine is implied. Newly authored visual source is delivered as part of the project.

The portable package includes the official Godot 4.5.2-stable license, third-party notices and author list under licenses/. Sources: https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.5.2-stable/LICENSE.txt and https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.5.2-stable/COPYRIGHT.txt .
